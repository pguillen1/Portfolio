Reading Tracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 16, 2026

Reading Tracker is an application developed by Pedro Guillén for managing books, reading progress, reading sessions, notes, quotes, and personal reading statistics. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Reading Tracker.

Information We Collect

Reading Tracker does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data with the developer or with third parties.
The application does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide personal information such as your name, email address, telephone number, or location.

Information Stored on Your Device

The information you enter into Reading Tracker is stored locally within the application's private storage on your device. This information may include:

  • Books added to your library.
  • Reading status and progress.
  • Reading sessions.
  • Reading duration and pages read.
  • Personal notes and quotes.
  • Application preferences.
  • Statistics generated from your reading activity.
The developer does not have access to this information and does not store it on developer-operated servers.

Device Backups

Depending on your device settings and operating system, Android may include some Reading Tracker data in system-managed backups or device-to-device transfers.
These backup services are controlled by Android, Google, or the device manufacturer and are associated with the user's own device or account. Reading Tracker and its developer do not directly access, manage, or receive the contents of these backups.
Users can manage their backup preferences through their device settings.

How Information Is Used

Information entered into Reading Tracker is used only to provide the application's features on the user's device, including library management, progress tracking, notes, reading sessions, and statistics.
Reading Tracker does not:

  • Sell personal data.
  • Share personal data with advertisers or other third parties.
  • Use personal data for advertising.
  • Track users across applications or websites.
  • Use analytics or advertising services.
  • Send user-created content to developer-operated servers.

Device Permissions

The current version of Reading Tracker does not request sensitive permissions such as access to:

  • Location.
  • Camera.
  • Microphone.
  • Contacts.
  • External storage.

Data Retention and Deletion

Reading Tracker retains information locally while the application remains installed or until the user deletes it.
Users can delete individual books, reading sessions, notes, and quotes using the options provided within the application.
Users can also delete all locally stored Reading Tracker data by:
Clearing the application's data through Android settings. Uninstalling the application.
The developer cannot recover deleted information because no copy is stored on developer-operated servers.
System-managed backups may be retained or removed according to the user's Android or Google backup settings.

Children's Privacy

Reading Tracker is not specifically designed for children under the age of 13.
The application does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any other users.

Data Security

Reading Tracker stores its data within the private application storage provided by Android.
The application does not transmit user-created content to developer-operated servers. However, no method of electronic storage can guarantee absolute security.

Third-Party Services

The current version of Reading Tracker does not integrate advertising, analytics, social networking, or third-party tracking services.
System services provided by Android, Google Play, or the device manufacturer may operate independently under their own terms and privacy policies.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Reading Tracker's functionality or data practices change. Any changes will be published on this page, and the “Last updated” date will be revised accordingly.
