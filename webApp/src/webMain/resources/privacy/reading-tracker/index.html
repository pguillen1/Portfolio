Reading Tracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 16, 2026

Reading Tracker is a local-first app designed to help users track books, reading sessions, notes and reading statistics.

Data collection

Reading Tracker does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data. The information entered by the user is stored locally on the user's device.

Data stored on the device

The app may store book information, reading progress, reading sessions, notes, quotes and preferences locally on the device.

Internet access

The current version of Reading Tracker does not require an online account and does not send user data to a server.

Third-party services

The current version of Reading Tracker does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs or tracking services.

Children

Reading Tracker is not specifically directed to children.

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the app changes how it handles data, for example if future versions add online features or integrations with external APIs.
